# Tai Lieu Chuyen Giao Chuc Nang OptiTax/EON

## 1. Tong Quan

OptiTax/EON la he thong ho tro ho kinh doanh va doanh nghiep nho quan ly ho so kinh doanh, khach hang, san pham, kho, hoa don dau vao, hoa don dau ra, chi phi, nhan su, luong va nghia vu thue.

Tai lieu nay tap trung vao danh sach chuc nang va flow su dung o goc nhin nghiep vu. Tai lieu khong mo ta cau truc ky thuat, ma dung de chuyen giao cho khach hang, PO, BA, QA hoac doi van hanh.

## 2. Nhom Nguoi Dung

### 2.1. Nguoi Dung/Doanh Nghiep

Nguoi dung chinh cua he thong. Nhom nay co the:

- Quan ly ho so doanh nghiep.
- Quan ly khach hang.
- Quan ly san pham va kho.
- Quan ly hoa don dau vao, hoa don dau ra.
- Theo doi chi phi, nhan vien, phieu luong.
- Theo doi han ke khai thue va tong hop so lieu thue.
- Cau hinh va thao tac voi EasyInvoice neu doanh nghiep co su dung.

### 2.2. Ke Toan Vien

Nhom nguoi dung co ho so nghe nghiep rieng. He thong cho phep quan ly:

- Thong tin giay phep/chung chi.
- Kinh nghiem lam viec.
- Chuyen mon.
- Hoc van.
- Lich su cong viec.
- Muc phi theo gio.
- Trang thai san sang lam viec.
- Danh gia va tieu su.

### 2.3. Quan Tri Vien

Quan tri vien co quyen quan ly va theo doi toan he thong:

- Quan ly nguoi dung.
- Quan ly doanh nghiep.
- Quan ly ke toan vien.
- Xem du lieu hoa don, kho, san pham cua tung doanh nghiep.
- Xem thong ke tong quan toan he thong.

Luu y nghiep vu tru kho:

- Neu mat hang tren hoa don trung voi hang trong kho, he thong tru truc tiep so luong ton cua hang do.
- Neu don vi tren hoa don khac don vi trong kho, he thong su dung quy doi don vi da thiet lap.
- Neu so luong ton khong du, he thong thong bao de nguoi dung bo sung hoac dieu chinh ton kho.

## 4. Flow Su Dung De Xuat

## 4.1. Flow Khoi Tao Tai Khoan

1. Nguoi dung dang ky tai khoan bang email va mat khau.
2. He thong gui email xac thuc.
3. Nguoi dung bam link xac thuc email.
4. Nguoi dung dang nhap vao he thong.
5. Nguoi dung chon vai tro hoac ngu canh su dung.
6. Nguoi dung cap nhat thong tin ca nhan neu can.

## 4.2. Flow Thiet Lap Ho So Doanh Nghiep

1. Nguoi dung vao khu vuc ho so doanh nghiep.
2. Nguoi dung nhap thong tin doanh nghiep: ten, ma so thue, dia chi, nganh nghe, so dien thoai.
3. Nguoi dung khai bao tan suat ke khai thue theo thang hoac theo quy.
4. Nguoi dung luu ho so.
5. Neu doanh nghiep su dung EasyInvoice, nguoi dung cau hinh thong tin tai khoan EasyInvoice.
6. He thong luu cau hinh va dung thong tin nay cho cac thao tac hoa don ve sau.

## 4.3. Flow Chuan Bi Du Lieu Ban Dau

1. Nguoi dung tao danh sach khach hang.
2. Nguoi dung tao danh sach san pham.
3. Nguoi dung khai bao dinh muc nguyen vat lieu cho san pham neu co.
4. Nguoi dung tao danh muc hang hoa/nguyen lieu trong kho.
5. Nguoi dung nhap so du dau ky cho kho.
6. Nguoi dung kiem tra danh sach kho va dieu chinh thong tin neu can.

## 4.4. Flow Dong Bo Hoa Don Dau Vao

1. Nguoi dung dang nhap hoac cung cap thong tin phuc vu dong bo hoa don.
2. He thong lay danh sach hoa don dau vao theo khoang thoi gian.
3. He thong kiem tra hoa don da ton tai hay chua.
4. Hoa don moi duoc luu vao he thong.
5. Hoa don da ton tai duoc bo qua.
6. He thong lay chi tiet hang hoa/dich vu tren hoa don neu co.
7. Nguoi dung xem ket qua dong bo: thanh cong, bo qua, that bai.

## 4.5. Flow Dong Bo Hoa Don Dau Vao Vao Kho

1. Nguoi dung mo danh sach hoa don dau vao da dong bo.
2. Nguoi dung chon dong bo hang hoa tu hoa don vao kho.
3. He thong doc danh sach hang hoa/dich vu tren hoa don.
4. Neu hang chua ton tai trong kho, he thong tao hang moi.
5. Neu hang da ton tai, he thong cap nhat so luong/gia tri lien quan.
6. He thong ghi nhan lich su dong bo.
7. Nguoi dung kiem tra danh sach hang moi can phan loai.
8. Nguoi dung phan loai hang la nguyen lieu hoac hang hoa.

## 4.6. Flow Lam Sach Du Lieu Kho

1. Nguoi dung xem danh sach hang trong kho.
2. Nguoi dung phat hien cac hang bi trung ten, khac ten nhung cung ban chat, hoac khac don vi.
3. Nguoi dung chon hang chinh can giu lai.
4. Nguoi dung chon hang can gop.
5. Nguoi dung nhap he so quy doi neu don vi khac nhau.
6. He thong cong ton kho vao hang chinh.
7. He thong luu ten goi khac/alias de cac lan dong bo sau nhan dien dung hang.
8. He thong xoa hang trung da duoc gop.

## 4.7. Flow Tao Hoa Don Dau Ra Va Tru Kho

1. Nguoi dung tao hoa don dau ra.
2. Nguoi dung nhap thong tin nguoi mua va danh sach hang hoa/dich vu.
3. He thong tu dien thong tin nguoi ban tu ho so doanh nghiep.
4. He thong tu sinh so hoa don, ky hieu va ma hoa don.
5. He thong tinh thue GTGT va TNCN theo tinh chat hang hoa/dich vu.
6. He thong kiem tra hang trong kho.
7. Neu hang la san pham co dinh muc, he thong tinh so nguyen vat lieu can tru.
8. Neu hang la hang ton kho truc tiep, he thong tinh so luong can tru.
9. Neu don vi khac nhau, he thong ap dung quy doi don vi.
10. Neu ton kho du, he thong tru kho va luu hoa don.
11. Neu ton kho khong du hoac thieu quy doi, he thong thong bao loi de nguoi dung dieu chinh.

## 4.8. Flow Phat Hanh Hoa Don Qua EasyInvoice

1. Nguoi dung dam bao doanh nghiep da cau hinh thong tin EasyInvoice.
2. Nguoi dung tao hoac chuan bi du lieu hoa don.
3. Nguoi dung chon thao tac nhap hoa don len EasyInvoice.
4. Neu can phat hanh ngay, nguoi dung chon nhap va phat hanh hoa don.
5. He thong gui du lieu hoa don sang EasyInvoice.
6. Nguoi dung co the xem lai hoa don.
7. Neu hoa don sai hoac khong con hop le, nguoi dung co the huy, xoa hoa don chua ky hoac dieu chinh hoa don tuy trang thai.

## 4.9. Flow Quan Ly Chi Phi

1. Nguoi dung tao phieu chi.
2. Nguoi dung nhap ngay chi, nhom chi phi, mo ta va so tien.
3. He thong luu phieu chi vao doanh nghiep tuong ung.
4. Nguoi dung xem danh sach phieu chi theo thoi gian.
5. Nguoi dung cap nhat hoac xoa phieu chi neu can.

## 4.10. Flow Quan Ly Nhan Vien Va Luong

1. Nguoi dung tao ho so nhan vien.
2. Nguoi dung nhap thong tin ca nhan, chuc vu, ngay vao lam, luong co ban va tai khoan ngan hang.
3. Den ky tra luong, nguoi dung tao phieu luong cho nhan vien.
4. Nguoi dung nhap tong tien va so tien da thanh toan.
5. He thong tu tinh so tien con lai.
6. He thong kiem tra trung phieu luong cua cung nhan vien trong cung thang.
7. Nguoi dung theo doi, cap nhat hoac xoa phieu luong neu can.

## 4.11. Flow Theo Doi Va Ke Khai Thue

1. Nguoi dung vao khu vuc thue.
2. He thong hien thi ky ke khai gan nhat, han nop va so ngay con lai.
3. Nguoi dung xem tong hop thue theo thang, quy hoac nam.
4. Nguoi dung tao ban ghi ke khai thue khi thuc hien nghia vu thue.
5. Nguoi dung nhap ma ke khai, ngay, mo ta, so tien va ghi chu.
6. He thong luu lich su ke khai.
7. Nguoi dung co the xem lai, cap nhat hoac xoa ban ghi ke khai.

## 4.12. Flow Quan Tri He Thong

1. Quan tri vien dang nhap vao he thong.
2. Quan tri vien xem dashboard thong ke tong quan.
3. Quan tri vien xem danh sach nguoi dung, doanh nghiep, ke toan vien.
4. Quan tri vien co the tao, cap nhat, xoa mem nguoi dung hoac doi vai tro nguoi dung.
5. Quan tri vien mo chi tiet tung doanh nghiep de xem hoa don, kho, san pham va thong ke thue.
6. Quan tri vien theo doi so lieu hoa don dau vao/dau ra va thong ke he thong theo nhu cau van hanh.

## 5. Cac Bao Cao Va Man Hinh Nen Co

Tai lieu nay suy ra tu cac chuc nang hien co. Khi thiet ke giao dien hoac chuyen giao cho khach hang, nen to chuc thanh cac man hinh sau:

- Dashboard tong quan doanh nghiep.
- Ho so doanh nghiep.
- Cau hinh EasyInvoice.
- Khach hang.
- San pham.
- Kho.
- Lich su kho.
- Bao cao ton kho.
- Hoa don dau vao.
- Dong bo hoa don dau vao.
- Hoa don dau ra.
- Thue va han ke khai.
- Phieu chi.
- Nhan vien.
- Phieu luong.
- Ho so ke toan vien.
- Quan tri nguoi dung.

## 6. Ghi Chu Chuyen Giao

- Moi du lieu nghiep vu cua doanh nghiep duoc gan voi ho so doanh nghiep tuong ung.
- Nguoi dung nen tao ho so doanh nghiep truoc khi su dung cac chuc nang hoa don, kho, san pham, nhan su va thue.
- Du lieu kho phu thuoc nhieu vao ten hang, don vi tinh va quy doi don vi. Can huong dan nguoi dung lam sach du lieu kho sau khi dong bo hoa don.
- De bao cao ton kho chinh xac, nguoi dung nen nhap so du dau ky truoc khi phat sinh nhap/xuat.
- De tru kho tu hoa don dau ra chinh xac, san pham can co dinh muc nguyen vat lieu hoac ten hang tren hoa don can khop voi hang trong kho.
- De su dung EasyInvoice, doanh nghiep can cau hinh day du thong tin tai khoan va serial.
- Quan tri vien co the dung cac man hinh thong ke de theo doi muc do su dung he thong va ho tro tung doanh nghiep khi can.
